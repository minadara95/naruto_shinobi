KINJUTSU Reality Rewriting

Izanagi (Reality Rewriting)

The ultimate Yin–Yang Release genjutsu cast upon oneself. Within its active duration, any injury or even death experienced by the user is turned into an illusion, while advantageous outcomes become reality. Price: The eye loses its light forever.

Wielders: Madara, Obito, Danzo Blinds 1 Eye
KINJUTSU Infinite Destiny Loop

Izanami (Infinite Destiny Loop)

Created to punish Izanagi abusers. Records physical sensations of both caster and target through sight to trap the victim in an inescapable temporal loop. The target can only escape by abandoning denial and accepting their true self and fate.

Wielders: Itachi, Naori Blinds 1 Eye
